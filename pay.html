Infrastructure Grade Payments

Built for power users who demand security, speed, and full control.

100% Non-Custodial Architecture

Every developer gets an isolated HD Wallet derived from a unique seed. Payments sweep automatically into your cold receiving address. We never hold custody — the private keys live encrypted in your account, not on our servers.

Zero Hidden Fees

No percentage cuts. No subscription gotchas. You only pay the tiny native gas fee for the on-chain sweep transaction.

Telegram Alerts

Get instant Telegram notifications the second a payment is verified on-chain. Low gas warnings too.

Developer-First REST API

Create invoices, check payment status, sweep funds, and manage wallets — all through a clean, documented REST API. Integrate into any stack in minutes. The BotFusion dashboard provides live analytics, usage graphs, and invoice history out of the box.

Payment Flow

From invoice to settlement in four automated steps.

01

Create Invoice

POST to /bfpay/invoices with the amount. You get a unique one-time deposit address.

02

Customer Pays

Customer sends USDT to the deposit address. The blockchain is monitored automatically.

03

Auto Sweep

Once verified, funds are swept from the deposit address to your master cold wallet automatically.

04

Instant Alert

You receive a Telegram callback and webhook confirmation. Claim funds at any time.
